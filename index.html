    Instructor controls

    Enter target readings, then apply immediately or transition over the selected duration. Traces remain connected when targets change. HR and RR control cycle timing throughout the transition. Select Linked ventilation to vary ETCO₂ inversely with RR; this simplified relationship assumes unchanged breath volume, metabolism, and perfusion. The manual mode preserves independent scenario values. Values can be set independently for scenario design; selecting a rhythm does not prescribe a matching heart rate. “None” represents a disconnected signal. Asystole/VF show no numeric ECG rate; ventilation remains independently controlled.
    Version 10 · NSR P wave hidden at HR ≥150 bpm as a training display setting. · Normal ETCO₂ I:E timing is 1:2: inspiration occupies one-third of each cycle at low CO₂; expiration occupies two-thirds with the upstroke and plateau. Other patterns retain their existing timing. Total cycle follows RR. · Waveforms are simplified training illustrations, including heart-block patterns. CPR starts at 10 mmHg (a lower target, not confirmation of adequate compressions); adjust to the intended scenario. ROSC starts with an abrupt rise to 55 mmHg; this above-normal value is a scenario choice, not a required ROSC finding. Rebreathing progressively raises the inspiratory baseline. Select linked ventilation to adjust ETCO₂ with RR, or use manual ETCO₂ for independent control. Capnography patterns alone do not diagnose cardiac arrest or ROSC. MAP is estimated as (SBP + 2 × DBP) / 3. Default alert limits are training settings, not clinical guidance. References: AHA adult ALS · Capnography waveform reference.
